Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Anah Switzer

Leading a female-owned boutique practice, Dr. Anah Switzer offers elevated, patient-centered care with expertise in same-day crowns, dental surgery and dental implants — combining precision, innovation and efficiency.

We love caring for the whole family and welcome patients of all ages, encouraging little ones to tag along early so they grow comfortable with the dental environment well before their own first visit.

Using state-of-the-art technology — Primescan digital impressions, CEREC restorations, digital and panoramic X-rays, intraoral cameras and gentle laser dentistry — we deliver high-quality care with integrity, always putting your comfort first.
